Two short checks using the existing Morphing split/merge solver, unchanged.

Intermediate fragments and finite-grid text edges are visible in the current result. These are direct checks of existing behavior, before a full text sequence.

Build details

Glyph geometry comes from the existing LaTeX engine. The unchanged biological Morphing library prepares a one-to-two lineage and the existing compiled GPU kernels interpolate fields, form contact necks and calibrate area. No entrance offsets, opacity fades or custom motion are added. The first glyph or word is the seed; empty geometry is not supported.

1080 × 1080, 48 fps, 193 frames per film. Software GPU capture; no hardware performance claim.
