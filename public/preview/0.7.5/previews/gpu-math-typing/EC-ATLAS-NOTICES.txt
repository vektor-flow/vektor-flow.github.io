TS1 micro/degree glyphs originate from the EC/TC METAFONT sources by Joerg Knappen and Norbert Schwarz. Their complete unmodified distribution is supplied as ec-source.zip, with copyrite.txt reproduced in EC-FONTS-NOTICES.txt. The generated, differently named atlas.rgba.gz contains outline representations extracted from real LaTeX output through dvisvgm; it is not an EC or TC font replacement. No original EC/TC files were modified. Atlas errors should be reported to the Vektor Flow preview maintainer, not the original font authors. Other tile fonts have their own included notices.
