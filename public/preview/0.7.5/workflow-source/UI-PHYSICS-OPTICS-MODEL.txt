POLARIZATION / ENERGY / UNITS
Four real Stokes components [I,Q,U,V] represent a per-ray incoherent ensemble:
Q=Is-Ip, U=2Re(Es Ep*), V=+2Im(Es Ep*). This explicitly chosen V sign differs
from some conventions. Ideal filters absorb rejected power without renormalizing.
Zero intensity requires zero polarization; relative validity protects dim signals.
The local interface basis is s perpendicular to incidence plane and p completing
its transverse basis. Call rotate_stokes for a changed plane. Coplanar lens/ray
examples share s, so they need no guessed 3D basis transport. Reflection includes
signed Fresnel cross terms; TIR retains relative complex phase. No phase coherence
between separate rays, diffraction, interference, spectral dispersion, absorbing
complex-index solids or general birefringent specimen is supplied. POM here means
a filter/analyzer and isotropic-interface bench, not a complete microscope.
Lossless real-index interfaces use Rs/Rp and Ts=1-Rs,Tp=1-Rp as ray POWER fractions,
not field amplitudes or an unexplained n-squared radiance factor. I is watts only
when input source I is watts. Room normalized light intensity is a separate model.
Lens first transmitted branch records omitted reflected power; ghost paths are
not recursively traced. Circle hit tolerances target metre-scale examples; no
arbitrary nano-optic scale robustness claim. Angles are radians in laws and degrees
only on plot axes. Geometric coordinates are explicit SI metres where labelled.

METHOD CHOICES, PRIMARY REFERENCES AND COST
NIST SCATMECH documents Stokes/Mueller and Jones representations. Four real Stokes
values support unpolarized and partly polarized sources; Jones alone represents
fully polarized coherent fields. Sparse filter/interface formulas avoid a stored
16-value Mueller matrix per event. Full wave grids would solve different physics
and are not a justified replacement for these ray experiments.
https://pages.nist.gov/SCATMECH/docs/stokesvector.htm
https://pages.nist.gov/SCATMECH/docs/elements.htm
PBRT's authors derive geometric reflection/refraction and dielectric Fresnel laws.
Exact smooth-interface coefficients give Brewster behavior; replacing these with
an unpolarized approximation would lose the s/p minimum. Its microfacet chapter
motivates a future GGX energy-aware renderer, while this minimal patch retains
existing Blinn behavior and declares its limits.
https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Reflection_Models/Specular_Reflection_and_Transmission
https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Reflection_Models/Roughness_Using_Microfacet_Theory
Edmund Optics' ball-lens equations independently give EFL=nR/[2(n-1)] and
BFL=EFL-R in air. R=1,n=1.5 has near-axis focus x=1.5 and first-branch central
power .96^2=.9216. Larger apertures have spherical aberration, not universal focus.
https://www.edmundoptics.eu/knowledge-center/application-notes/optics/understanding-ball-lenses
Existing elastic source uses six generalized DOFs, consistent mass and implicit
midpoint, with real small-strain/rotation checks and an energy/work ledger.
This preserves a meaningful contact/virtual-work seam. It is not a full tree,
large-deformation FEM or a scalar spring standing in for bending. EX04 derives
wind drag loads from prescribed air speed; it does not solve an air pressure field.
Existing rigid replay remains the current backend, rather than a host replacement.
Its source admission/runtime availability, friction/rolling and multi-contact
behavior must be verified for the exact scene candidate.
For EM, the current Lorentz force rate remains explicit. Primary Boris-integrator
work compares phase accuracy and structure preservation, useful if a particle
pusher is actually integrated; this source does not add Maxwell/PIC or rename
polarization as full EM.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.04378
For fluid motion, the current Newtonian local rate requires supplied pressure,
advection and boundaries. Cheap analytic cylinder potential flow has a slip
boundary and cannot honestly replace viscous water around irregular rough rocks.
EX03 now supplies this restricted analytic circular-slip reference field, coupled
through the EXISTING dimensioned fluid_velocity_rate and momentum integrator.
It uses a round rock boundary with procedural shading roughness; it does not
claim rough irregular/no-slip boundary flow or a general pressure solver.
https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/fluids/fluids1.pdf
Diffusion flux/rate similarly require actual spatial operators. Those laws and
existing unit rejection controls remain preserved, not silently declared complete
spatial simulations or hidden under an optics-only coverage claim.
