Isolated VKF optics test with no stone, splash, foam or droplets. One finite-depth plane-wave packet crosses the chamber, reflects at the far wall and returns. Refraction uses the actual resolved surface normal, measured water IOR and Snell's law—without normal attenuation or an artistic refraction-strength control. A separately rendered mirror camera is composed with exact unpolarized Fresnel reflectance. This chamber has no sky model: the reflected colour comes only from the retained red/black, blue/black and green/black walls plus the emissive sun. Missed rays see the black scene background. The neutral dark checker floor remains visible through refraction. This is a development proof, not a released feature.
