Preparing the shared symbol atlas…

Supported: Latin letters and digits; all lowercase and uppercase Greek; large sums, products and integrals (including contour integrals); relations and angle brackets; nested fractions, scripts and power towers; nested \left / \right; matrix, pmatrix, bmatrix, Bmatrix, vmatrix, Vmatrix, smallmatrix, cases, aligned and array. Accents include hats, tildes, vector arrows and dots; overlines/underlines, annotated overbrace/underbrace, overset/underset and labelled xrightarrow/xleftarrow are available. Units support \qty / \unit, \SI / \si, \num and \ang with common SI prefixes, powers and per-mode options. Unfinished input updates immediately with empty operand placeholders and inferred closing scopes. Unknown commands show a placeholder. Preview recovery leaves your input unchanged. Inline uses compact fractions and side limits; Display uses large operators and stacked limits. Explicit \displaystyle, \textstyle, \limits and \nolimits can override these defaults.
