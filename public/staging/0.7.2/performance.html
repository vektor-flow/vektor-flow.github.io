Remaining compiler and harness gaps

macOS ARM retains private ABI argument overflow and oversized list-literal backend failures. ARM and Windows parallel core entry timers fail. WASM retains unreachable traps in linear algebra and symbolic expansion, an incorrect add1 result (0 instead of 2998), unavailable internal timers and unverified concurrent execution. Additional Intel retains capacity failures and rejected zero timers. Symbolics expand1 times out at its original limit on Linux, ARM and Windows; this peer failure cannot be credited as a successful full comparison. Each exact platform/workload diagnostic remains in the linked matrix and failures.

Original failures and partial evidence

The raw reports retain all partial valid samples from failed workloads, rejected zero timers, numerical outcomes and exact failure diagnostics. A missing peer, trap, timeout or unverified concurrent execution cannot earn full comparison credit. Original relative-speed gates remain separate from numerical correctness.
